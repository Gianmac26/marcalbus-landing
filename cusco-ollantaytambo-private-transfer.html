FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a private transfer from Cusco to Ollantaytambo cost?

The rate depends on the vehicle you need and the number of passengers. Send us your group size and travel date and we will reply with a quote.

Do you take us to the Ollantaytambo train station?

Our service takes you to Ollantaytambo so you can catch your train to Machu Picchu. Tell us your train time and we will plan the transfer around it and confirm the drop-off details in your quote.

Can you wait for us or pick us up when we return?

Return pickups and round trips can be arranged. Tell us when you will be back and we will include it in your quote, subject to availability.

Which vehicle should I choose for my group?

Mini Van for up to 7 passengers, Mercedes-Benz Sprinter for up to 19, Mini Bus for up to 25 or 33, and Bus for up to 45. For larger groups we combine vehicles. Luggage can change the recommendation, so tell us how many suitcases you have.

Is a guide available?

A guide is available on request and is quoted according to your group. Tell us you would like one and we will add it to your quote.
