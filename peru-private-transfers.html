FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a private transfer cost?

Rates depend on the vehicle type, number of passengers, route and number of days. Send us your details and we will reply with a quote.

Can you change or extend the route?

Yes. For example, a Lima - Paracas - Ica - Nazca trip can continue to Arequipa if your group wants. We can also add stops or build a route from scratch.

What vehicles do you offer?

Mini van for up to 7 passengers (Hyundai H1 type), Mercedes-Benz Sprinter for up to 19, minibuses for up to 25 or 33, and buses for up to 45. All with air conditioning and full equipment.

Do you cover other routes?

Yes. Besides the routes listed, we can plan airport transfers, city transfers and other destinations in Peru. Tell us where you want to go.

Do I need to tell you my luggage?

It helps. The number of suitcases can change the vehicle we recommend, especially for groups.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can, especially in high season (roughly June to September). Last-minute requests are welcome and we will check availability.

Is the service private?

Yes. The vehicle and driver are exclusively for your group during the trip.
